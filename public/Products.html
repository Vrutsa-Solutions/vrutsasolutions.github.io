SoCreate

Share ideas. Build audiences. Earn from day one.

SoCreate reimagines how creators publish and earn online. Unlike platforms that lock monetization behind subscriber counts and watch-hour thresholds, SoCreate opens the door from day one — whether you're a student, a professional, or a business building thought leadership.

KEY FEATURES
  • Publish & discover ideas with a built-in social feed
  • Creator subscriptions — Go Premium and Creators Pro tiers
  • Revenue Pool Sharing — 50% of subscription revenue to creators
  • AI-powered tools for writing, summarizing, and categorizing
  • Direct messaging and a real-time creator dashboard

TheyDi

Find your people. Join real gatherings.

In a world of endless group chats and scrolling, meaningful in-person connections are harder to find than ever. TheyDi creates a dedicated space to discover gatherings, host your own events, and build genuine local communities.

KEY FEATURES
  • Discover local events by category, date, and distance
  • Host gatherings with a request-to-join approval system
  • My Events dashboard — hosting, attending, past events
  • In-app event chat, ratings, and trust scores

NotesCase

Your notes. Your passwords. One secure place.

NotesCase brings sticky notes, saved passwords, Wi-Fi credentials, and software licenses into one clean, secure, instantly accessible app. Sign in with Google and your notes are encrypted, synced, and available wherever you need them.

KEY FEATURES
  • Organized tabs — notes, passwords, Wi-Fi, licenses
  • Built-in password generator and quick-copy fields
  • Google sign-in with real-time Firestore sync
  • Full-text search across every tab
  • Privacy-first — no ads, no third-party data sharing
